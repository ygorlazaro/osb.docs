# Namespaces

OSLANG provides built-in namespaces and primitive/namespace methods for common operations. Availability depends on language/runtime version.

## Common namespaces (by era/version)
- **Math & primitives:** `MATH`, string/number/array helpers (e.g. `SQRT`, `POW`, `TYPEOF`, conversions) — expanded across 0.4–0.6+
- **Filesystem & environment (host-dependent):** `FILE`, `DIR`
- **Time:** `DATE`, `TIME` (0.5+)
- **Internationalization:** `I18N` (where provided)
- **Data interchange (0.62+):** `OSL.JSON`, `OSL.CSV`, `OSL.XML`, `OSL.CNF`
- **Networking (0.62+):** `OSB.NET` (e.g. `PING`, `DOWN`)
