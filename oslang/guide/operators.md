# Operators

OSLANG supports arithmetic, comparison, logical, and (in newer versions) extended operators. Exact precedence and availability are version-specific.

## Typical categories
- **Arithmetic:** `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `MOD`, `**` (power, version-dependent)
- **Comparison:** `=`, `<>`, `<`, `>`, `<=`, `>=` (forms per spec)
- **Logical:** `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`
- **Assignment/compound (later):** `+=`, postfix `++/--` (0.6+)
- **Other:** string concatenation/interpolation and member/index access as defined by the spec
